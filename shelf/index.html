The one that isn't cheap

I bought the Ooni, not the KitchenAid.

It's a spiral mixer. The hook turns while the bowl turns under it, which works the dough the way hands do instead of dragging it around a stationary bowl — less friction, less heat, and it handles wet dough without climbing the hook and stalling.

It's around $800, which is more than everything else on this page put together. I'm not going to pretend that's an impulse buy. But I was making four batches a week by hand and the KitchenAid everyone recommends was going to be the wrong tool twice.

If you're baking two loaves on a Sunday, skip this entirely. The list above is the real list.
